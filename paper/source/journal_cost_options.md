# Journal Cost Options for Contact-EC

Checked on 2026-08-09. Fees can change, so confirm inside each submission
system before final submission.

## Current Primary Target

### BMC Bioinformatics

- Fit: high for this manuscript because the paper emphasizes scientifically valid
  benchmark analysis, sequence analysis, structural bioinformatics, machine
  learning, and reproducibility.
- Cost model: fully open access.
- Listed APC: GBP 2290 / USD 3090 / EUR 2590 for accepted articles, plus taxes
  where applicable.
- Payment timing: after acceptance, not at initial submission.
- Cost-reduction options: institutional open-access agreement, country-tiered
  pricing, or discretionary waiver/discount requested at the point of submission.
- Practical advice: submit here if a waiver/discount or institutional support is
  possible, or if paying the APC after acceptance is acceptable.

## Possible Lower-Cost or No-Author-Fee Alternatives

### Computers in Biology and Medicine

- Fit: moderate. It includes bioinformatics and computational biology/medicine,
  but the manuscript may need stronger biomedical framing and clearer relevance
  beyond enzyme benchmark methodology.
- Cost model: hybrid. Elsevier lists an optional open-access APC, but the
  subscription publication route is listed as no publication fee charged to
  authors.
- Indexing caution: the journal's current Guide for Authors includes a research
  integrity notice stating that Web of Science Core Collection coverage for
  content after Volume 172 (2024) is not currently indexed. Do not use this as a
  primary target if SCIE indexing is a strict requirement.
- Practical advice: this is a possible no-APC route only if subscription
  publication is acceptable and indexing risk is acceptable. It should not
  replace BMC Bioinformatics as the primary target for this manuscript.

### Journal of Biomedical Informatics

- Fit: low to moderate. It is methodologically strong, but expects clear
  biomedical or clinical problem motivation and comparison to the state of the
  art. Contact-EC would need a broader trustworthy biomedical AI framing.
- Cost model: hybrid. Elsevier lists an optional open-access APC, but the
  subscription publication route is listed as no publication fee charged to
  authors.
- Practical advice: a better-indexed no-APC fallback than Computers in Biology
  and Medicine, but the current enzyme-function benchmark manuscript is not a
  natural fit without substantial reframing.

### Artificial Intelligence in Medicine

- Fit: low to moderate. The journal is more medical AI focused than molecular
  bioinformatics focused.
- Cost model: hybrid. Elsevier lists optional open-access APC, but the
  subscription route is listed as no publication fee charged to authors.
- Practical advice: not the best first target for Contact-EC unless the story is
  substantially reframed around trustworthy AI evaluation.

### Journal of Computational Biology

- Fit: moderate for computational biology, but the paper may need a stronger
  algorithmic or benchmark-methodology framing.
- Cost model: not free in practice. The journal states there are no submission
  fees, but accepted manuscripts have mandatory page charges of USD 90 per page.
- Practical advice: lower total cost than BMC for a 13-page manuscript, but not
  zero-cost.

### iMetaOmics

- Fit: uncertain. It includes bioinformatics tools, pipelines, and packages, and
  currently advertises waived APCs, but prestige and long-term fit should be
  checked carefully before choosing it over BMC Bioinformatics.
- Cost model: currently no cost to publish while APCs are waived.
- Practical advice: consider only as a backup if cost is the dominant constraint
  and indexing/scope are acceptable.

## Not Cost-Free for This Manuscript

- Bioinformatics: fully open access with APCs for original articles, though
  waivers/discounts and institutional agreements may apply.
- Bioinformatics Advances: fully open access; expect APC/waiver workflow.
- Briefings in Bioinformatics: fully open access; expect APC/waiver workflow.
- PLOS Computational Biology: APC applies unless covered by institutional
  agreement, Research4Life, or fee assistance.
- Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal: open access APC applies.

## Recommendation

For acceptance probability and topic fit, keep BMC Bioinformatics as the primary
target and request APC waiver/discount at submission if needed. If a no-APC route
is mandatory, the safest checked route is a hybrid subscription journal, not a
fully open-access bioinformatics journal. Journal of Biomedical Informatics is
better indexed but lower fit; Computers in Biology and Medicine has better topic
fit but a current indexing caution. For this manuscript, BMC Bioinformatics with
a waiver/discount request remains the best balance.
